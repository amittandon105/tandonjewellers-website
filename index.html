About Tandon Jewellers

Trusted in Samastipur since 1991

Tandon Jewellers is a family jewellery showroom in Babeja Market, Gudari Bazar Chowk, near Hanuman Mandir, Samastipur, Bihar. For over 35 years we have helped families choose gold, silver and diamond jewellery for weddings and everyday life, along with gemstones, rudraksha and shankh, explained in person.

Visit us from 11 AM to 7 PM (closed on Tuesday), call or WhatsApp +91 62046 01253.

Since when has Tandon Jewellers been serving customers?

Do you offer gemstone and astrology guidance?

Yes. Share your birth details through the consultation form on this website and our team will reply on WhatsApp with guidance as per traditional Vedic belief. This does not replace medical, legal or financial advice.

Which gemstone is linked with which weekday in tradition?

By tradition: Sunday Ruby (Manik), Monday Pearl (Moti), Tuesday Red Coral (Moonga), Wednesday Emerald (Panna), Thursday Yellow Sapphire (Pukhraj), Friday Diamond (Heera), Saturday Blue Sapphire (Neelam).
